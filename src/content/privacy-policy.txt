Daily Nova Privacy Policy
Effective date: October 6, 2026

1. Overview
Daily Nova is a personal productivity app for notes, tasks, habits, diary entries, journal entries, and reminders. This policy explains how information is handled when you use the app.

2. Personal content stored on your device
Your notes, tasks, habits, diary entries, journal entries, reminder settings, tags, and attachments are stored locally on your device. Daily Nova does not require an account and does not automatically upload or sync this personal content to our servers.
You control exports and sharing. When you export or share content, a copy may be stored outside the app or sent to an app or recipient you choose. Your device or operating system may also include app data in backups, depending on your settings.
Local storage does not mean that every app feature operates without internet access. Advertising, online voice transcription, quote retrieval, and feedback submission may communicate with external services as described below.

3. Google AdMob advertising
Daily Nova uses Google AdMob to display advertisements, including rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may collect or process advertising identifiers, IP addresses, device and app information, ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and, where permitted, personalize ads.
Advertising data is separate from your locally stored notes, diary, journal, tasks, and habits. Daily Nova does not send that personal content to AdMob for advertising.
Consent requirements and advertising controls vary by region and device. You can manage available advertising privacy settings on your device. Learn more at https://policies.google.com/privacy and https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. Permissions and their purposes
Notifications: To deliver task, habit, diary, and journal reminders. On supported Android devices, exact-alarm and full-screen alert access may be used for timely alarms and strong reminder alerts.
Camera: To take photos that you choose to attach to reminders or other supported content.
Photos, media, and storage: To select attachments and to save, import, export, or share files. Access depends on your Android version and may use a system file or photo picker rather than broad storage access.
Location: To obtain your device's location for place-based reminders that you enable. Location reminder coordinates are stored on your device and are not sent to our servers. Location checks depend on permissions, operating-system restrictions, and whether the app is allowed to run.
Microphone and speech recognition: To turn speech into text when you press the microphone control. Online recognition may send audio to the browser's or device's speech-recognition provider for processing; the provider's privacy policy applies. Daily Nova does not operate its own audio storage service.
Biometric authentication: If enabled and supported, to request device authentication for the app lock. Daily Nova does not receive or store your fingerprint or biometric template.
You can decline optional permissions or change them in device settings. Features that depend on a declined permission may not work.

5. Voice transcription
Voice input starts only when you choose to activate it. Daily Nova does not use a wake word or intentionally listen continuously in the background. Recognized text is added to the content you are editing and stored locally when you save it.
The recognition service depends on the browser or device implementation. Online transcription requires internet access and may process audio outside your device. It is not ChatGPT's transcription service, and Daily Nova does not promise the same accuracy or privacy practices as ChatGPT.

6. Quotes and external requests
When online quote retrieval is used, a quote service and the service hosting the request may receive ordinary connection information, such as your IP address and request metadata. Your diary, journal, notes, and other personal entries are not included in quote requests.

7. Feedback and contact
When you submit feedback in the app, the message is sent to our hosted feedback service when a connection is available. The submission may include your device platform and app version. A local copy or pending submission may remain on your device so failed submissions can be retried.
Please do not include passwords or sensitive personal information in feedback. If you contact us by email, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. Submitted feedback and contact messages are used to respond to requests, investigate problems, and improve the app.
Contact: dailynovasupport@gmail.com.

8. Sharing and sale of information
Daily Nova does not sell your notes, diary entries, journal entries, tasks, habits, or attachments. Information may be processed by service providers for advertising, online speech recognition, quote retrieval, or feedback submission, as described in this policy. Information may also be disclosed when legally required.

9. Retention, deletion, and your choices
Local app content remains on your device until you delete it, clear app data, or uninstall the app, subject to your device's backup settings. Any Recently Deleted feature may retain items until you permanently delete them. Exported files, shared copies, and device backups are not automatically removed when you delete content from Daily Nova.
Feedback and contact messages may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to address your request, operate the service, and meet legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of submitted feedback or contact information, subject to applicable law and our ability to identify the submission.
Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of personal information. Contact us to make a request. For data processed independently by Google or a speech-recognition provider, use that provider's privacy controls or request process.

10. Security
We aim to limit collection of personal content and use device-based storage for your productivity data. App locks can help limit casual access but are not a guarantee of encryption or security. No device, storage method, or transmission system is completely secure. Protect your device, keep it updated, and handle exported or shared files carefully.

11. Children
Daily Nova is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 through feedback or contact submissions. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. A higher minimum age may apply under local law.

12. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy when features, services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will show a revised effective date. Material changes will be communicated in the app when appropriate.
